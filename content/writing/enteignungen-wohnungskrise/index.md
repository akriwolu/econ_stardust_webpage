##### Summary

Written after the Berlin state election of September 2026, in which Die Linke became the strongest party on a platform of expropriating large landlords and capping rents. Berlin's problem is one of quantity: too few homes have been built for years, construction has become expensive through regulation, and more than 100,000 homes are missing. Expropriation adds none and would tie up tens of billions of euros in compensation that could fund schools and universities instead. We argue for converting offices into flats, deemed approvals after fixed deadlines, activating designated building land and giving investors reliable rules.
