##### Summary

Written when inflation first took off in early 2022 and calls for state-administered prices grew louder. Price controls do not remove the cause of inflation; they suppress the signal that tells producers and consumers how to adjust, create shortages and shift the costs elsewhere. The piece explains why they risk achieving the opposite of what they promise.
