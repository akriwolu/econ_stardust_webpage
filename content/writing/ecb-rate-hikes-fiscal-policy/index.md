##### Summary

A single interest rate for twenty countries, but twenty different fiscal reactions. Using data since 1999, we estimate how euro area governments respond to ECB rate hikes and what that means for output and inflation. Countries that cut spending after a hike, such as Germany, Italy and Greece, lose roughly twice as much output as countries that keep spending, such as Spain, Belgium and Ireland, while inflation comes down more slowly in the latter group. With the reform of the debt brake and the large infrastructure and defence packages, Germany is likely moving from the first group to the second, so the ECB may have to tighten more, or for longer, to bring inflation down.
