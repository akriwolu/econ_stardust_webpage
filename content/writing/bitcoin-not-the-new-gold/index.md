##### Summary

Bitcoin is often sold as "digital gold": a scarce asset that protects investors when markets fall. We compare ten years of monthly returns of Bitcoin, gold, US and German stocks and government bonds. Gold is essentially uncorrelated with stocks and bonds, which is precisely what makes it useful for diversification. Bitcoin, by contrast, moves strongly with the stock market, so it offers little protection when it is needed most. Whatever Bitcoin is, it is not the new gold.
