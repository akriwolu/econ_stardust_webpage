##### Summary

Renewable energy and climate-resilient infrastructure need large upfront investment, which makes them particularly sensitive to interest rates. We argue that the ECB should offer lower refinancing rates for climate investment, so that fighting inflation does not come at the expense of the transition that would make Europe less vulnerable to the next energy price shock.
