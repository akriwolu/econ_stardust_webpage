##### Summary

Published while CDU/CSU and SPD were negotiating the 500-billion-euro infrastructure fund. Our model simulations suggest the package lifts GDP by about one percent in 2026 and by more than two percent per year on average over the following decade, mostly because public investment crowds in private investment: the output multiplier is roughly two. Inflation rises only moderately, by about half a percentage point per year, and financial market reactions give little reason to fear fiscal dominance in the euro area.
