##### Summary

Five weeks after the Russian invasion of Ukraine, we simulated an immediate stop of Russian energy imports. German GDP would fall by about three percent at the trough, roughly eighteen months in, with effects lasting for years, and inflation would rise by up to 2.3 percentage points; without a suspension of the EU deficit rules the decline would be closer to four percent. The key message is that fiscal policy would be in charge: targeted spending, transfers and investment could substantially limit the downturn, and the government should prepare for a supply stop rather than hope it never comes.
