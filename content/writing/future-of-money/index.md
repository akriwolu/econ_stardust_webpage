##### Summary

Written for the European Parliament's Committee on Economic and Monetary Affairs when Facebook announced Libra. We ask whether stablecoins issued by large technology companies threaten the money we use today, and what central banks should do about it. Rather than banning private money or building a retail digital euro from scratch, we propose a public-private model: central banks cooperate with stablecoin issuers to create a "synthetic" central bank digital currency that combines the technology of the private sector with the safety of central bank money.
