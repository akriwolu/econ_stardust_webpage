##### Summary

Central banks like to say that they cannot do much about energy-driven inflation. We identify three channels through which monetary policy affects energy prices, some of which pull in opposite directions, and estimate their combined effect for the euro area. The net result is clear: when the ECB raises its key interest rates, energy prices fall. This is the policy piece behind our research paper "Friend, not foe: Energy prices and European monetary policy".
