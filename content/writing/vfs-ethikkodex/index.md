##### Summary

In December 2021 the members of the Verein für Socialpolitik, the association of German-speaking economists, voted to extend its code of ethics beyond standards for scientific work to principles of good professional practice: inclusion, non-discrimination and a working environment free of hostility. As members of the working group that drafted the extension, we explain the motivation, the research on the disadvantages women and minorities still face in economics, and how the code is meant to work in practice.
