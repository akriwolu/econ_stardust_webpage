##### Summary

The longer analysis behind our newspaper commentary. We put numbers on the proposal to socialise the roughly 240,000 flats owned by large housing companies in Berlin: depending on the level of compensation, the interest rate and the politically set rent, the annual burden on the state budget runs into the billions, while the housing stock does not grow by a single unit. The calculator lets you change every assumption yourself; all sources and the underlying spreadsheet are provided.
